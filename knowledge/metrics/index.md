# Metrics

* [M1: most duplicated museum name](m1-most-duplicated-museum-name.md) - Headline count of counted, non-chain museum institutions sharing one canonical L2 name.
* [M2: Singularity Collision Index](m2-singularity-collision-index.md) - For names carrying a scope claim (International, National, World …), the count of independent institutions sharing the L2 name.
* [M3: genericity split](m3-genericity-split.md) - Partition museum names into asserted-unique and descriptive and report the two distributions separately.
* [C1: most duplicated church name](c1-most-duplicated-church-name.md) - Headline count of church records sharing one L3 name_core.
* [C2: territory radius](c2-territory-radius.md) - Distribution of nearest-neighbour great-circle distances between same-name congregations, per name_core class and denomination.
* [C2: municipal exclusivity rate](c2-municipal-exclusivity.md) - Of Census places with at least one congregation of a denomination, the fraction holding exactly one First church of it.
* [C3: ladder completeness](c3-ladder-completeness.md) - For each place with a maximum ordinal N, the fraction of ordinals 1..N present.
* [C3: maximum ordinal](c3-max-ordinal.md) - Highest ordinal observed, by denomination and by city, with the top handful verified by hand.
* [C4: naming-culture profile](c4-naming-culture-profile.md) - Distribution of name_style per denomination: ordinal, saint, virtue, toponym, modern brand and others.
* [C5: naming over time (post 3)](c5-naming-over-time.md) - Cohort analysis of name_style by founding decade on the subset with reliable dates; deferred to post 3.
* [Municipal multiplicity list (post 4)](municipal-multiplicity-list.md) - Places holding two or more First churches of a denomination, emitted as a named artifact for post 4.
