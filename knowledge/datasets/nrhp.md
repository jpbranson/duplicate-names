# Role

Construction and listing dates for historic churches from the NPGallery bulk spreadsheet (90k+ properties). It skews old and architecturally notable: a biased but datable sample. NRHP nominations often narrate the post-Reconstruction congregation splits post 4 needs.[^design] Founding-date acquisition is deferred to post 3
([decision 3](../decisions/03-temporal-c5-deferred.md)); see the
[data sources overview](overview.md).
