How to read this explorer

Museum headlines use L2. Eligible museum candidates exclude known chains, but unknown affiliation remains unknown. L3 strips recognized geography and answers a different question. One reviewed canonical museum record represents each institution; source aliases remain visible.

Church counts use L3 and represent provisional Overture worship sites. GNIS and HIFLD are comparison sources, not additional counted congregations. Independent matching, classifier and cluster reviews remain pending. Distant campuses are not automatically combined.

Blue means eligible for the selected analysis, not factually verified. Orange holds stay inspectable. A verified review status records completed factual review; it is not an independent estimate of matching accuracy. No OSM fields are included. Census 2023 outlines provide context without remote tiles.
