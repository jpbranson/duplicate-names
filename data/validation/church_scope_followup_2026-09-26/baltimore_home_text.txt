Isaiah 56:7 – “for mine house shall be called a house of prayer for all people.”
Welcome to the webpage of First Tabernacle Beth El Baltimore, MD. We are happy that you are taking the time to visit with us. God’s people are called to be one family and that is how we worship, as one family. Together we call the spirit of our Almighty God through singing, praising, testifying and worshiping. We thank the God of our salvation for waking us up each morning and starting us on another day’s journey. We entreat God’s people with the power of “Thus saith the Lord God.”
Please note our address and come worship with us. Experience devotion to God as you have never experienced it before. Come to know praise of God as you have never praised before. Feel free to uplift yourself and others testifying of the goodness of God to the children of men. Come …worship with us, make our family of worship your family of worship.
We look forward to worshiping with you soon.
