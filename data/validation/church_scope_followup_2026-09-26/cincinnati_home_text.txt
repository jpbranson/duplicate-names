Welcome to the website of Seventh Tabernacle Beth El, Cincinnati, Ohio. We are happy that you have taken the time to visit with us. We are a small church with a rich history. We are God’s people, called to be one family and that is how we worship, as “one family”. Together we acclaim the Almighty God through singing, praising, testifying and worshiping. We thank the God of our salvation for waking us up each morning and starting us on another day’s journey. Using God’s word as the foundation of our faith, we entreat God’s people with the power of “Thus saith the Lord God.”
Our hope is that you will stop by 3944 Cass Avenue and worship with us. Experience a cappella singing, soul-stirring sermons and devotion to God as you have never experienced them before. Come to know praise of God and feel free to uplift yourself and others testifying of the goodness of God to you and the children of men. We offer opportunities for all ages to grow in spiritual understanding through teaching, preaching, servicing others, and fellowship. We welcome you to come to Seventh Tabernacle Beth El and worship with us, make our family of worship your family of worship. We can’t wait to see you! We look forward to worshiping with you real soon!
