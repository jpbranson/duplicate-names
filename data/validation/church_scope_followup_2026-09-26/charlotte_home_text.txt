Welcome to the home page of Second Tabernacle, Charlotte, North Carolina. Whether you’re visiting for the first time or a returning friend please know we appreciate the opportunity to share information about us and are honored to have you interact with us.
Our Tabernacle was organized in the Queen City in 1906. Built on the foundation of the Prophets and Apostles – We are unique; a peculiar treasure as a congregation with a deep desire to love one another and serve God. Our services have a strong focus on the teachings of the Bible with goals to glorify God, adherence to the Ten Commandments, as well as, helping members foster a deeper relationship with God and to shed light as we all journey on this spiritual path.
Let our A cappella choir soothe your soul with sweet songs of Zion as we invite you to leave your burdens behind and celebrate in knowing (Psalms 118:24) “This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.” Together, we will make a joyful noise unto the LORD!
Join us on our spiritual pilgrimage, as we strive to become the people that God would have us to be. Come and be inspired by dynamic preaching and teaching of “Thus Saith the Lord GOD.”
Accept this as our personal invitation to you – Our Guest.
Come! Stop by to worship and fellowship! Let us exult in the Lord together!
Mission
The mission of Second Tabernacle is to be a place for prayer and worship, enabling and fostering community and spiritual development, undergirded with a dynamic God-consciousness.
