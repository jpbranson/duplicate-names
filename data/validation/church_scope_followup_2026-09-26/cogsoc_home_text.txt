Selah Cafe / Encouragement
AT THE CHURCH OF GOD AND SAINTS OF CHRIST, EVERYONE IS WELCOME.
We are a church where people matter; come fellowship with us as we grow in God's love.
The Church of God and Saints is a church where people matter. We’re a church on the rise! We gather for fellowship and teaching; we sing together and pray together. Through worship and study, we grow and discover God’s purpose for our lives.
We are a community of individuals called to a higher purpose. The message of Jesus Christ and His love brings us together, gives us purpose, and defines who we are and what we are about.
God loves each of us and wants us to have a closer relationship with Him. Wherever you are on your faith journey, we welcome you to be a part of our fellowship.
Being a believer isn’t complicated…it’s simply putting faith and trust in Jesus Christ into action. We hope you will join us on this journey and experience the unconditional love and freedom that comes with knowing who God is.
