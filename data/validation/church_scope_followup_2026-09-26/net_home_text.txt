Explore Our Site
As Senior Bishop and Chief Commander of the Church of God and Saints of Christ, Head-quarted in Cleveland Ohio, it is with extreme pleasure that I both welcome and invite you to explore the website of the Church of God and Saints of Christ founded and re-established in 1896 by Bishop William Saunders Crowdy, a Prophet sent directly by God according to the Holy Scriptures (Malachi 4:5).
Your visit will reveal Spiritual visibility throughout the USA, Bermuda, Canada, England, Jamaica, South Africa, Mozambique and Malawi. I personally invite you to visit one of our locations; your experience shall be like none other.
May the peace of God and the freshness of His Holy Spirit rest in your thoughts and conquer all fear. May God reveal his-self in ways you never believed possible. May your joys be fulfilled and your prayers answered. May the Lord God of Heaven and of earth and the world to come bless and keep thee; the Lord make His face shine upon thee and be gracious unto thee; the Lord God “lift up” His countenance upon thee and give thee everlasting peace!
