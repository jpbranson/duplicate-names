Welcome to Fifth Tabernacle Beth El, Atlantic City, NJ
Established in the early 1900s, our congregation has been serving the Atlantic City region for more than a century through preaching, teaching, fellowship and outreach. Our goal is to serve, love, and support all mankind so that we can be a blessing to this community. We sincerely believe that through human betterment, true fellowship and deeds of kindness we can have a closer walk with God and help others to do the same. When we do that, we will have accomplished the mission set forth by our re-establisher, Prophet William Saunders Crowdy.
Please know that our doors are always open to you and we hope that you will visit us soon!
“…For mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people.” Isaiah 56:7, KJV
