Welcome to the homepage of Fourth Tabernacle Beth El – Richmond, VA, affectionately known as “The Strong Tower”.
We are honored to have you visit this website. Our Israelite congregation has served the Greater Richmond area since 1902. Fourth Tabernacle Beth El is a family, joined together by God’s love and a genuine desire to assist with the needs of our fellowman.
Our mission is to develop disciples to spread the Word of God to those in need of inspiration and encouragement through our God-centered worship services, rich heritage of our acapella singing, biblically based preaching and teaching and a holistic approach to community outreach programs.
If you are ever in Central Virginia, we invite you to join us during one of our impactful services each week on the Beginning of the Sabbath (Friday evening at 7PM) and Sabbath Day (Saturday at 11:30AM).
From the ministers, officers and members of Fourth Tabernacle Beth El, your spiritual family: we look forward to worshiping with you!
“The name of the Lord is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it and is safe.” – Proverbs 18:10
