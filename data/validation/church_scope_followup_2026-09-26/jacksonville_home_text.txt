History of the Tabernacle
Jacksonville. The Tabernacle with the Golden Doors. The Saints with the Golden Hearts.
Our mission is to exemplify love for the Source of All Life, the I AM THAT I AM, by loving our neighbors as ourselves. As it states in 1 John 5:3 “For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments:” We understand that the only way one can show that they love God is by keeping his commandments (Exodus 20:1-17), and by treating others as they would like to be treated. (1 John 4: 20)
Our religious organization adheres to the tenets of Judaism. Anchored in the principles of a love for God, a respect for character and a yearning for wisdom, we embrace the faith of our forefathers as recorded in the Bible.
If you live in or are visiting the area, we welcome you to stop by to experience this great love.
