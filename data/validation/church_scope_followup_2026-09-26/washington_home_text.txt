Welcome to First Tabernacle Beth El in Washington, D.C., known as Jerusalem, the city of peace. We believe through prayer, true fellowship and deeds of kindness, we strive to know God for ourselves and establish a direct link to His supreme power. Our tabernacle, known by the neighborhood as the little white church on the corners of 4th street and New York Avenue, welcomes all people to join us on our spiritual journey.
We invite you and your family to join us to be spiritually uplifted and gifted with an inspiring word of God. We encourage you to stay and listen to the sweet songs of Zion from our a cappella choir. Allow us to extend an opportunity to you to worship in an environment that is energized by a desire to give ALL glory to God. Come join our family who thrives in an atmosphere where social and economic status does not define us.
We are children of God who live according to His purpose.
