Our Gospel Ministry
The Church of God and Saints of Christ was re-established in November 1896 by Prophet William Saunders Crowdy who was Elijah in the spirit. The local churches in Jamaica are found in the parishes of St Thomas, St. Mary, St. Catherine, St. James, Hanover, Westmoreland and St. Elizabeth. The international body is made up of tabernacles located in The United States of America, South Africa, Bermuda, The Cayman Islands, England and Canada. Bishop Robert D. Grant, Chief Executive Officer, is the pastor of the church in Cleveland, Ohio.
