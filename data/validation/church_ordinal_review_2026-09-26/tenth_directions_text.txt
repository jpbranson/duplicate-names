Directions
Tenth is located on the corner of 17th and Spruce Streets in Center City Philadelphia. Public transit is readily available, with more than a dozen routes within a 10-minute walk.
During business hours, our office entrance is at 1701 Delancey Street.
For evening and weekend events, note the entrance indicated by the hosting ministry.
Sunday Street Parking
Get a Placard
Parking Garages
Nearby garages offer reduced rate or free parking for Tenth attendees are generally a better parking option on Sundays since street parking fills up quickly.
Sunday Times and Locations
Limited street parking (with a Tenth Parking Placard; see conditions below) is available for all Sunday Worship Services at the following times and locations, on both sides of the street:
Sundays: 8:00am — 2:00pm on east/west streets:
Note that this is NOT available for the Sunday Afternoon Service, as that service continues after 2pm.
North side of Lombard from 16th to 19th
Sundays: 8:00am — 10:00pm on north/south streets
East side of 16th from Pine to Latimer
East side of 17th from Latimer to Spruce
East side of 17th from Spruce to Pine (for people with accessibility needs only, guidelines below)
West side of 18th from Lombard to S. Rittenhouse Square
Installation Service ONLY (Sunday, September 20, 2026): 4:00pm — 10:00pm

The city may ticket or tow vehicles that are parked:
In handicapped spaces.
Within 5 feet of curb cuts for driveways or garages.
In bus stops.
In bike lanes.
On any sidewalk.
Within 15 feet of fire hydrants.
Within 20 feet of corners.
Without Tenth placards, which are available
online
and at the Spruce Street entrance.
More than 1 foot from the curb.
Tenth Parking Placards
You will need to display a Tenth Parking Placard on the dashboard of your vehicle (driver’s side), visible through the front windshield. Placards are available
online
and at the Spruce Street entrance.
Parking Safety and Courtesy
When parking on the street we ask that you…
Check carefully for bike riders before opening your door into traffic.
Display the current parking placard in your front windshield.
Get a printable placard here
or from the kiosk in the Spruce Street entrance.
Familiarize yourself with the Sunday street parking guidelines. Not following these guidelines may result in your car being ticketed or towed.
Accessible Parking
Accessible parking for those with limited mobility for Sunday services is available on 17th Street from Spruce to Pine.
An “Accessible” Tenth placard is required.
Signs and blue cones may be placed as a reminder. Follow all other Sunday street parking guidelines found above.
Get a printable accessible placard here
or from the kiosk in the Spruce Street entrance.
Other Street Parking
Many of the blocks around tenth have street parking which is free and without time limit on Sundays.  Always check the signs.
Parking Tickets
If you receive a parking ticket, please take a picture showing where and how you were parked.
Confirm you were parked within the allowed areas, listed here and on the back of the parking placard.
Email your photo and a scan or picture of your ticket to
[email protected]
.
Staff will review the ticket and equip you with information you need to resolve it as easily as possible.
Keep the original ticket in your personal files until the matter is resolved.
Tickets are your responsibility.  We want to help, but cannot help resolve tickets if they are not submitted to us promptly. Tenth will not reimburse ticket or towing fees.
Garage Parking
Penn Garage
1700 Block of South Street
Enter from South Street.  The entrance is on your right in the middle of the block.
Stamp your ticket at Tenth’s Delancey or Spruce Street entrances. You must have exact change after 8pm.
Saturdays and Sundays
: $6 with stamped ticket
Weekday Events (approved events only; must enter after 5 PM)
: $6 with stamped ticket
Ez Park 15th & South
1441 South Street (Sundays, any duration)
Stamp your ticket at Tenth’s Delancey or Spruce Street entrances.
Sundays
: $15 with stamped ticket
Saturday & Weekday Events
: Not available
Liberty Place Garage
between 16th—17th, Market—Chestnut (Sunday services and some special events only)
Park in the garage taking the ticket from the machine as usual. After the worship service, bring your ticket to the Welcome Center, who will place a sticker on the back of it. Exit the garage on either 16th Street (northbound) or 17th Street (southbound). Scan the garage ticket first and then turn it over to the sticker side scan the payment sticker.
Sundays
: Free with sticker on your ticket
Saturday & Weekday Events
: Not available

Other Options
While we only have negotiated rates with the lots above, you can often find reasonable parking rates by booking online in advance with tools like

Special Event Parking
Special street parking is available for some events where it has been coordinated in advance with the city.  Please check the event details page for the event you’re attending to see if Special Event Parking is available.

Special Event Times and Locations
