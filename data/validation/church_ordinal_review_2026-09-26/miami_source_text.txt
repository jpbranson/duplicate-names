Helpful details
Frequently Asked Questions
Where will the banquet be held?
The Celebratory Banquet will be held at Aqua Reception Hall, 14060 SW 8th Street, Miami, FL 33184, approximately eight miles from the host hotel.
Is parking available?
The host hotel offers complimentary self-parking. Final parking instructions for each event venue will be shared with registered guests.
What is the banquet attire?
The Saturday evening banquet is an elegant celebration. Formal or after-five attire is encouraged.
Which airports are closest?
Miami International Airport and Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport both serve the Miami area.
When is the souvenir journal deadline?
Souvenir journal advertisements, artwork, and payment are due by December 5, 2026.
