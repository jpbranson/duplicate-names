Welcome to the Tenth Tabernacle Beth El, Miami, FL
Organized in the Magic City in 1961, we are a spiritual oasis along Florida’s Gold Coast. There is much to enjoy in our beautiful corner of the world, from beautiful beaches, vibrant business, cultural diversity, and exciting night life. But, there is nothing quite as special as when the saints praise the name of the LORD in the beauty of holiness. Nothing compares to the sublime acts of outreach where we demonstrate our faithfulness to the commandment, “Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.”
We invite you to come and worship with us, and to join us on our spiritual pilgrimage, as we strive to become the people that God would have us to be. We invite you to hear beautiful acapella singing of the sweet songs of Zion. We invite you to be inspired by dynamic preaching of “thus saith the Lord GOD.” And, we invite you to be part of our spiritual family that Prophet William S. Crowdy established.
