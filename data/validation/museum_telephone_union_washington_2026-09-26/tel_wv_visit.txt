Telephone Museum
Fairmont, WV
304.276.5445
214 Monroe St. Fairmont, WV 26554
Take a step or two back in time to understand the intricacy of communication's past. The museum contains various switchboards, pay phones, booths, test boards as well as other telephone equipment. Tours are led by knowledgeable people who worked in the industry.
1000 Cole Street, Suite A
Pleasant Valley, WV 26554
Phone: 304-368-1123
info@marioncvb.com
