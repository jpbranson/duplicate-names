Exploring Union County's Past to Inform the Present and Future
Welcome!
For over a hundred years, the Union County Historical Society has promoted the history of Union County, Pennsylvania through educational programs, displays, publications, events, and celebrations. We invite visitors to explore our research library at the courthouse, which includes genealogical resources, history publications, and microfilm newspaper archive, as well as providing research services.
​
The Society offers programs at the newly restored Gallery at 15 N. Water Street, Lewisburg, and provides tours of the 1793 Dale-Engle-Walker House and Kantz Log Cabin, Strawbridge Rd., Lewisburg. Check the website calendar and newsletters for details.
