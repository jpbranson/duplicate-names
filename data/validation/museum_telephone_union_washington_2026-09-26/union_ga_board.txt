Our Board
The Union County Historical Society wouldn't be able to function without the tireless efforts of our volunteers.  Our Board of Directors is comprised of thirteen individuals:  four officers & eleven members.  Without their collective input and direction, we wouldn't be able to achieve our goal of promoting and preserving Union County's rich history.
​
Meetings are held on the second Tuesday of each month at noon at the Mock House Annex, one block south of the Square.  Meetings are open to the public.
