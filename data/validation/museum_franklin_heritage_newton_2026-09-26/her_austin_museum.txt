Heritage Center Museum
A museum for items of historical significance and an archive of the school’s films, photographs, and written records are maintained and circulated for display in the Heritage Center, located at the heart of the TSD campus.
If you would like to visit the Heritage Center,
please contact us to send the request for the date and time.
Contact:
https://tsdalumni.org/contact-us
