About us
Founded in 1949, The Newton County Historical Society is a local 501(c)(3) non-profit dedicated to preserving and sharing the stories of the people, places, and events that have made Newton County unique.
Our purpose and mission is to preserve the rich and diverse history of Newton County, Missouri and make it available for all to share.
The Newton County Historical Society welcomes everyone with a passion for history! Whether you're a lifelong resident, a recent newcomer, or just visiting, we offer a variety of ways to connect with the stories that have shaped Newton County.
