Purpose of the Ashbel Woodward Museum Board
The purpose of the Ashbel Woodward Museum Board is to provide leadership, oversight, and stewardship for the museum in support of its mission to preserve, interpret, and share local and regional history. The Board works to ensure the long-term sustainability of the museum by guiding policy, supporting collections care, approving programs and exhibitions, and fostering strong relationships with the community. Board members advocate for the museum, support fundraising and strategic planning efforts, and help ensure that the museum remains an accessible, educational, and welcoming cultural resource for residents and visitors alike.
