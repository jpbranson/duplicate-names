We’d love to hear from you! The Franklin Historical Society is dedicated to preserving and sharing the stories that shaped our community—and your interest helps keep that history alive.
Whether you’re planning a visit, researching your family roots, or simply curious about Franklin’s past, we’re here to help.
How We Can Help
People contact us for many reasons, including:
Scheduling a tour or field trip
of our historic buildings and grounds in Legend Park.
Reserving facilities
such as the chapel, town hall, or outdoor spaces for weddings, photos, or private events.
Learning about membership and volunteer opportunities
—join us in preserving Franklin’s history and sharing it with others.
Requesting historical or genealogical information
, or asking about local landmarks and archives.
Donating artifacts, photos, or family histories
that contribute to our growing collection.
Inquiring about upcoming programs, events, or community partnerships.
We welcome residents, students, researchers, and visitors alike—anyone with an interest in learning about or supporting Franklin’s heritage.
