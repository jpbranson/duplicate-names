About Bedford
Bedford's History
You can take a walking tour and enjoy Bedford's historical charm while shopping at the unique stores in the Village, enjoying delicious food options, visiting us at The Bedford Store or just relaxing on the Green.
Stop into our office at The Bedford Store, 612 Old Post Road and pick up our
Walking Tour Book
and Map which can be purchased for $20. You can also buy just the map for a $1 donation.
Pick up our "Hunt for History" clues book which takes you through Bedford's three hamlets on a historical treasure hunt. Books are free and available in The Bedford Store. Or you can download your own
Clues Keeper here
and
email
us for the answer key!
The Bedford Historical Society is located at 612 Old Post Road in Bedford, NY in The Bedford Store building. We provide information on Bedford’s history and current events and host educational programs. We also operate a small gift shop offering our publications, Bedford gifts and more.
