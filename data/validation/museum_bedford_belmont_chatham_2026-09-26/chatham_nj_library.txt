Program Description
Event Details
To commemorate this year’s 250th anniversary of United States independence, The Chatham Historical Society, and the  Chatham Township Historical Society have created exhibits focused on the events of 1776. One panel highlights key events from the Revolutionary War era in Chatham and Morris County. Such events include the setting up of a Liberty Pole in Chatham (indicating the pro-independence stance of most community members, along with George Washington’s visit and stay in Chatham from August 27 -28, 1781. The other panel features information on the five delegates from New Jersey who signed the Declaration of Independence (Abraham Clark, Francis Hopkins, John Witherspoon, John Hart, and Richard Stockton).
This exhibit is part of the library's America250 series of events celebrating American history. Visit
chathamlibrary.org/america250
for more information.
