Welcome to the Belmont Historical Society, Inc. We enjoy helping the citizens of Belmont learn more about the rich and diverse history of the city that began its life as Garibaldi. The historical house museum has many permanent displays with furnishings, artifacts, photos, and many other things that show Belmont’s history from the earliest nomadic people who came through this area through the textile era.
Many Belmont citizens, past and present, are featured in photos on our walls, in scrapbooks, and in the Belmont Sports Hall of Fame.
