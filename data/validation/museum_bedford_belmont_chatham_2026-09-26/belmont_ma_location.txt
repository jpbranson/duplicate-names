Where We Are
Belmont Public Library
336 Concord Avenue
The Belmont Historical Society is located in the Belmont Public Library at 336 Concord Avenue.  The Society’s home and the location of its collections is the Claflin Room – go in the front entrance of the library, turn right at the cross-corridor, and you will see a sign for the Claflin Room.  Volunteers from the Society are in the room on Monday and Wednesday afternoons from 2:00 to 4:00 pm and on Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 7:00 to 9:00 pm to assist the public with browsing or research.
