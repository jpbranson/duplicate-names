Historic Site Management Partners
Ohio History Connection contracts with local nonprofit organizations and government entities for the day-to-day operations of several of its historic sites. A list of such organizations and their contact information appears below.
Per our current
Privacy Policy
, we may share information you provide us with these affiliated organizations in order to
create a cohesive visitor experience for our supporters
and patrons, and to allow staff at these organizations to verify patrons' eligibility for benefits they offer to Ohio History Connection members. These organizations are bound to protect your information in accordance with their own privacy practices. We recommend you contact the organizations using the information on this page to learn about their privacy practices.
