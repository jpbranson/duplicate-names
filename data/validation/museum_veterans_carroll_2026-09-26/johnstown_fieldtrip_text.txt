JOBS
The best field trip in the area!
Join us in our very successful EXPERIENCE ICE field trips, where students learn STEAM through the historic, 75-year-old arena!
SCIENCE –
Discover the science and chemistry of ice skating rinks
TECHNOLOGY
– Discover behind-the-scenes machinery used to create and maintain 17,000 gallons of frozen water!
ENGINEERING
— A look at the refrigeration systems and Zamboni!
ARTS/ACTIVITY
– Students can paint on the ice and go ice skating!
MATH/MILITARY
– Students receive age appropriate worksheets pertaining to arena and hockey math! In addition to visiting the military museum in the lobby!
Field trips available in Johnstown at 1ST SUMMIT ARENA and in Ebensburg at North Central Recreation Center.
Field trips are available from September to May, starting at $3 per student!
Over 35 field trips booked in the last two years!
