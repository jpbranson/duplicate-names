Brown County Historical Society
Welcome!
The Brown County Historical Society has created this website as a way for people living in Brown County, South Dakota, or those who have moved away or had friends or relatives from here to share their stories, history, and knowledge of the towns, ghost towns, events, and way of life through the years.
Project 150
Brown County Historical Society (BCHS) has created a new online history resource ahead to mark Brown County’s 150 years…Project 150. It’s an interactive website which, thanks to modern technology, is open to contributors both locally and worldwide. We encourage you to share multiple stories throughout your time in Brown County. Don’t be shy! Click the links below to get started or keep on scrolling.
