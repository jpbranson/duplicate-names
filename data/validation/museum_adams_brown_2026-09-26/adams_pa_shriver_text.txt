One family disrupted by war
The Shriver House Museum offers a rare glimpse into Gettysburg’s civilian history and its role in the Civil War.
From the harrowing experience of the Shriver family to the sharpshooters who occupied the attic, this restored home tells the full story of life during the battle.

Step into the Shriver House Museum, where the chaos of war collided with the lives of ordinary civilians.

The Shriver House
Travel back in time with a guide in period attire as you walk through the Shrivers’ meticulously restored 1860 home to learn the other side of the story – the civilian side of the Battle of Gettysburg. Connect to the past while you listen to the story of George, Hettie, Sadie (7) and Mollie (5) unfold as you move from room to room to appreciate what life was like before, during, and after the Civil War.
