About Us
Behind every exhibition, program and event at the African American Museum Dallas is a passionate team of curators, educators, historians and creative professionals dedicated to preserving and sharing the richness of African American culture.
Together, our museum staff brings vision, scholarship, and heart to everything we do — from curating groundbreaking exhibitions and hosting educational programs to welcoming guests and engaging our community.
We are storytellers, caretakers, and collaborators united by a shared purpose: to honor the past, celebrate the present and inspire the future.
