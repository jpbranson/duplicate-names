Getting There
Address
African American Museum Dallas
3536 Grand Avenue, Fair Park
Dallas, TX 75210
Free Parking
Free parking is available in the Fair Park lots. Enter through Gate 5. Special Event staff may direct you to other Fair Park gates. The museum is centrally located within the park.
Public Transit (DART)
The DART Blue Line stops at the Fair Park Station, a short walk from the museum entrance. Plan your route at dart.org.
Before You Arrive
Visitor Tips
✦ Allow 60–90 minutes for a full visit to all four galleries
✦ The exhibition is emotionally powerful — some content may be intense for younger visitors
✦ Photography is permitted in most areas of the exhibition
✦ Arrive at museum opening for the quietest experience
✦ Check aamdallas.org for special programming, curator talks, and community events
✦ The exhibition is fully wheelchair accessible with subtitled multimedia content
