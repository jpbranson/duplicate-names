The Origin Story
Imagination Station has an illustrious history and an eminent focus on the future. Throughout our history, our mission has remained strong because we value science as an integral part of our world.
Always Moving Forward
We have an unwavering commitment to serving our community by delivering unforgettable science experiences, building strong connections to STEAM careers and investing in dynamic, hands-on learning that challenges people of all ages to think critically and creatively. Imagination Station's goal is to meet the needs of our community while inspiring the next generation of innovators and problem-solvers.

2022
November 5
Guinness World Record
Imagination Station sets a Guinness World Record for collecting the most glass bottles for recycling in one hour: 20,987 lb 8.38 oz (9,519.78 kg). The record-breaking event took place during the Science of Guinness World Records exhibit. Guadalajara, Spain, held the previous record of 5,478 lb 7 oz.
