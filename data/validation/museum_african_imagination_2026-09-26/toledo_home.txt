Become a Member
Membership at Imagination Station offers unlimited access to hands-on science exhibits and engaging educational experiences all year round. Members enjoy exclusive benefits like free admission, special event invitations and discounts in Science2Go, Barry Bagels and Atomic Café. It’s a fun and affordable way for families to stay curious and connected to STEAM learning.
