Welcome to Imagination Station!
We are a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit, NAEYC-accredited Child Care and Learning Center located in Zeeland, Michigan.
At Imagination Station, children receive a well-rounded, faith-based education taught by professionals. Our focus is on the intellectual, physical, and spiritual growth and development of each child. We offer engaging programs for children six weeks of age through school age. You’ll be amazed at the difference we can make in your child’s early learning!

About Us
Friendly Staff
Imagination Station team members are carefully selected based on our philosophy and vision for our children and programs. Our staff is experienced, caring, sensitive to the needs of children, and holds the appropriate credentials.
NAEYC Accredited
The NAEYC works to improve the quality of early childhood learning programs. Imagination Station has achieved NAEYC accreditation, having demonstrated that it provides excellent educational programs and a safe and healthy environment for children.
Thematic Curriculum
We approach early childhood learning through a thematic curriculum that emphasizes hands-on experiences. Our curriculum is based on bi-weekly themes and is integrated into our learning centers, which foster love of learning through play.
15 Years of Experience
Since 2003, our goal has been to provide a “World-class child care program of excellence.” When you leave your children with us, you can be confident that they will receive the physical, emotional, social, and academic nurturing that they need.
