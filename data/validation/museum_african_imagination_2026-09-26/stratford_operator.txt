Imagination Station Welcomes New Board Member
•
Monday, September 13, 2021
We are excited to announce we have a new Board Member – Sherry Holubec!! (She replaced Sheryl who moved to Oklahoma)
Sherry so graciously volunteered to be part of our Board because she admires all the hard work we do for the children of our community. Sherry has been to several of our events and just happened to mention that she would love to help us out – boy did we took her up on that offer!
So here is a little bit about Sherry: she has been in Stratford for about a year permanently but commuted back and forth from south of Midland for eight years prior to that. She is married to Dennis (for 43 years!), has three daughters and nine grandchildren. She keeps books for the family business and recently has taken up quilting.
Sherry also enjoys playing Bunco and attending her grandchildren’s events.
We are so thrilled to have Sherry on our team- we know she will add so much to the Board. WELCOME!!
—

Imagination Station Introduces You To Their Volunteers--Laurie Spurlock
•
Tuesday, July 6, 2021
The ninth and last Spotlight Board Member is me – Laurie Spurlock.
I’m definitely the “old timer” of the board – I have been on for 20 years! I got on the board at the pivotal point where we were either going to make a better go of The Imagination Station or dissolve it completely.
Luckily for the community, we chose to keep it going! I love all the MEMORIES I have made with my family and friends down at Imagination Station.
My sweet husband, Brent, has been a hero, a pirate, a president and a not so very pretty lady (haha). My daughter, Mary Ann, has been with me to all Mother/Daughter brunches I’ve been at (and she is willing to be whoever she needs to be for them). My son, Russell, built the stage as his Eagle Scout project and what a wonderful addition that has been.
One of my favorite events was the melodrama “Fast Train to the Poorhouse” – I was the heroin and Brent was the villain. So many friends were in that play and we had waaaay too much fun at rehearsals (sorry Terri Teig) – but what a blast and lots of laughs! I love our Italian Dinner Fundraiser in January – it’s a time to really visit and thank you all for your support. Outside this Board, I keep books for the family farms (and help move equipment and go get parts, etc), serve on several other boards and I am on the Executive Council for my sorority alumni group.
I want everyone to know what a special place The Imagination Station is and how blessed we are to have it in our community. Please come join us at events or send your kids – you/they are the reason we exist!
And remember that we are a non-profit organization that runs off of donations.
I’m really thankful I am a part of this amazing and outstanding group!!
—

Imagination Station Introduces You To Their Volunteers--Nita Barnard
•
Monday, June 28, 2021
Nita Barnard is our next Spotlight Board Member.
We like to refer to her as “the cleaning machine”! She is so willing to go down after a hard day’s work and clean the kitchen, bathrooms, front room – whatever needs done!
She has been on the board for 10 years and has been extremely valuable. She thinks of the things that need to happen that the rest of us don’t think of – i.e. opening the cabinet doors when the weather gets too cold. She was the first one down there when our toilet backed up – plunging and mopping. She really does the thankless jobs but we are so appreciative that she is the one willing to do those things.
Nita’s job as a seed dealer for Pioneer keeps her extremely busy during the day, but she always provides snacks/supplies for events she can’t make it to. We are grateful she has a trailer and hay that is readily available for parades! Nita applies her business knowledge and dealings to our board and keeps us on the up and up.
Her favorite memory is the our 25th Gala Celebration – she loved her station in the front room, having it all decked out like “Breakfast with Santa” and serving yummy treats and drinks.
Breakfast with Santa is her favorite event – she cherished having her son, Bret, come join her by helping serve (for multiple years) when he was younger. She says the fun and excitement of the kiddos is a blast.
Thank you again Nita for serving on our Board!!
—

Imagination Station Introduces You To Their Volunteers--Sheryl Springer
via Facebook
•
Monday, June 7, 2021
Our next Board Member to Spotlight is Sheryl Springer.
Sheryl joined the board two years ago after she retired from teaching (34 years!). We like to think of Sheryl as our “liaison”. She connects us to the kid’s minds and understands what the teachers might want/need. Being on our board has kept her involved with kids and she now knows that the adult activities are just as fun.
Her favorite event is Breakfast with Santa – she loves watching the kids come in to a fun themed room and seeing Santa (whether happiness or horror)!
This year at our Mother’s Day craft event, Sheryl was extremely amused by helping the kids answer questions about their moms.
Sheryl goes all out with costumes and made the darn cutest “Lost Boy” at our Mother/Daughter Tinkerbell Brunch this last spring. Sheryl is also our “liaison” with the Thrift Store, for which she is also a volunteer. If we are in need of something for a prop or decoration, she will check the Thrift Store for us – and as a non-profit this is enormously helpful.
She says that being on our Board is a worthwhile endeavor as The Imagination Station is a wonderful place to be and so benefits our community. Sheryl also serves on the Education Foundation Board – she is a true champion of the children!
We are super lucky Sheryl chose to be on our Board – we are the benefactors of her hard work, great ideas and teacher qualities (she keeps us in check)!
Thanks Sheryl for all you do!!

Imagination Station Introduces You To Their Volunteers--RaChelle Long
•
Monday, May 17, 2021
RaChelle Long is our next Board Member Spotlight. RaChelle is definitely our “paper aficionado”! You give that girl a roll of paper and a projector and watch out!!
She has been on the Board since October of 2019. She was pretty unsure of what we did or how she would fit in when she came to her first meeting, but lucky for us she kept coming back. RaChelle has really had to be out of her comfort zone being on our board – she claims she would have never dressed up in public or held a snake if it weren’t for us!
She loves how a board member will throw out an idea and then we all run with it. Her favorite event is Breakfast with Santa – she said mainly because she has done that event twice and got to see how it changes from one year to the next. She worked hard at putting together our first ever Mother/Son Game Night back in February – look for this to become an annual event – and she loved play Bean Boozled (a game of secret flavored jelly beans) at it. She also brought in the most guests at our Mom’s Morning Out Painting event.
Her real job is working as the secretary for Mungia’s Heating and Air and she also participates in strength training competitions.
According to RaChelle “If you haven’t made it to an event at Imagination Station, you are missing out”.
Thanks RaChelle for your positive energy and problem solving skills – you are a true asset to the Board!

via Facebook

Monday, May 3, 2021
Our #5 Spotlight Board Member is Kay McAdams. Kay is our “magician” – she has a wonderful way of turning something from one thing into something else – and they are always amazing!
Coat hangers and panty hose turned into wings, cow horns turned into a cool decoration, some shelving turned into a jail... you get the picture.
Kay joined the board 4 years ago and always is willing to help with whatever needs done! She recently spent hours and hours painting the mural on the south wall. She loves working with the all the kids and really enjoys the events where she can dress up and pretend she is someone else – she is always super creepy looking at our haunted houses!
This year she had a blast being the Grinch for our "Whoville" Breakfast with Santa. She enjoys meeting people at all our different functions and just hanging around the board in general.
Outside Imagination Station, Kay owns a small clothing business (Katydids) and she chases her grandkids (and “adopted” grandkids) around – sporting events, rodeos, etc.
Thank you Kay for going above and beyond!

Imagination Station Volunteer --Barbara Haile
•
Monday, April 19, 2021
Spotlight #4 is Barbara Haile. We are so lucky this sweet lady rejoined our board about a year ago – she is definitely the “sweet one” and is pretty darn handy with a tree saw!! Thanks Barbara for your kindness to all! Here is her bio in her own words:
When I was growing up, I always swore that I would never live in a small town or reside where the wind blew all the time. However, even after all those years ago when I followed my heart to Stratford, I’ve never regretted my decision and have been here ever since. It was definitely a lesson in the importance of never saying “never”. I have loved living in the wild country of the Panhandle, raising my beautiful family, and renovating our 97 year-old home. One of these days I’m hoping I might actually get finished with the house, but that might be a little optimistic.
I’m most proud of my work as a teacher. To be able to work with kids has been the most fun and rewarding career path I could have ever chosen. For the first six months after I retired I would wake up in a panic thinking, “Oh, my word!!! What have I done?” So when I was asked to become an Imagination Station Board Member, I jumped at the chance because I was going to get to work with kids again!
This is actually my second time to be on the Board because I was one of the founding members of The Imagination Station (1992). Years ago when my amazing and forward-thinking friend, Kay Brizzolara Pronger, visited with me about an idea she had for developing a non-profit organization in Stratford that promoted science, history, art and encouraged children and adults to create and dream, I decided that she was a genius. My favorite memories are those working with her to bring this incredible concept to life for the people of Stratford and the surrounding area. I am so thankful for Kay, her vision and her determination. What a marvelous gift it has been for all of us to enjoy!
—

Imagination Station Introduces You To Their Volunteers--Jana Clift
•
Monday, April 5, 2021
Jana Clift is our next “Spotlight” – she is our “craft master extraordinaire”
We truly rely on her to come up with most of the crafts and a lot of our decoration designs. Thanks Jana for all your inspirations! Here is what she has to say:
My husband and I moved our family to Stratford 9 years ago in order to raise them on a farm. I love following our three daughters (Kaytly, Morgan, Ellie) around to all their activities, plus they get to volunteer at the Imagination Station.
I am the local real estate professional having my Texas Realtors license for almost 9 years where I work alongside my husband Bryan at Clift Land Brokers. I have volunteered on SSRC board, 4-H parent leader, and SISD committees.
I now serve on the Sherman County Child Welfare Board and the Imagination Station. I have been on the Imagination Station board for almost 5 years. I still remember when Laurie and Nita asked me to join, I seriously felt like the cool kid for once! LOL! I have always loved working with kids - whether helping with an educational project or letting them artistically express themselves with glitter and sequins.
That’s probably why I was so excited to join, I could be crafty and artsy myself but I get to experience it with kids as well and that is exciting! I don’t really have a favorite memory, but the Mother Daughter Brunch is my favorite event. This was one of the first events I experienced with my own girls before I joined the board.
To see our space get turned into something magical like “Under the Sea”, or “Mary Poppins”, or “Beauty and the Beast” and then to see all these moms and daughters experiencing these magical moments together is special.
I love dressing up and my girls get to volunteer and dress up too! Most importantly I have found a board of friends within the Imagination Station.
—

The Imagination Station is one of the true assets in the community of Stratford and has been for nearly 20 years!
It is a non-profit educational, just-for-fun kind of place that hosts exhibits, offers camps and various activities throughout the year.
Located at 202 N Main Street, this group of energetic volunteers make sure there is always something fun to do in your town!
The ""crew"" is a volunteer board that includes Jill Killian, Jana Clift, Laurie Spurlock, Nita Barnard, Kay McAdams, RaChelle Long, Barbara Haile, Sheryl Springer and Adrian du Plessis.
They are funded by donations and have couple of fundraisers each year.
