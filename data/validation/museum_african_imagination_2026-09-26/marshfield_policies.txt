OUR MISSION 
 
Welcome to Imagination Station where learning and fun go hand in hand!  We are a state licensed facility founded in 2006!  
We are committed to your family, providing a unique place designed just for kids! Our handmade and creatively constructed  
discovery and learning facility provides care and nurturing in a safe environment. 
 
At Imagination Station children lead the way through hands-on interactive fun promoting     
intellectual, social, emotional and physical health. An array of age-appropriate activities is available     
throughout our facility. We also offer after school homework help upon request. You will find no other place in Marshfield that     
provides such a high-quality and well-respected program as ours.

ARRIVAL/PICK-UP 
An adults must check-in and check-out students. We utilize a childcare program app called Brightwheel for check-in and check-out, group 
announcements, parent to teachers or director communication and more. You will be given an invite to join our school Brightwheel group.  
Please do not allow your child to come into Imagination Station unattended. Inform the teacher of your arrival and check your child in & 
out for attendance. Unless other written arrangements have been made, your child will be released only to you or to other individuals named 
by you on the child’s enrollment form. No child will be released to anyone under the influence of alcohol or drugs. 
 
SAFETY 
Safety is of utmost concern with all children at ISM. For this reason, we will keep our doors locked from outside visitors for 
your child’s safety and security. We provide a front doorbell to ring when picking up your child. 
 
• We have immediate access to a landline telephone and a minimum of one cellular phone in the case of an emergency.  
• Emergency drills will be conducted on a regular basis.  
• Emergency exit routes are posted throughout the facility.  
• A first aid kit is located in the front office. 
• School age and Preschool age students will have separate play spaces unless 10 or fewer children total are     
 remaining; in such cases multi-ages may be grouped together for activities. This usually occurs after 3:45pm daily. 
  
We are required by Missouri State law to report all incidents of suspected or actual abuse or neglect of children regardless of 
whether they occur during or outside of school hours.  
 
ILLNESS/INJURY 
• Children may not attend if they are ill or have a fever of 99 degrees or higher within a 24 hr. period of scheduled 
attendance.  Please respect the health of others. 
• We have a “No Nit” policy.  A doctor’s release stating that ALL individuals in your household have been lice & nit 
free for at least 1 week is required before returning to Imagination Station. 
• Parents/Guardians of students enrolling or enrolled at Imagination Station may request notice at any time during 
enrollment of students currently enrolled having immunization exemption on file. 
• If your child is injured or becomes ill at ISM, we will: 
1. Notify you immediately. 
2. Follow your instructions if emergency medical care is needed. 
3. Note the date and the description of any accident or injury in your child’s facility record. 
 
 
DISCIPLINE POLICY 
• Children will follow simple, understandable rules. We use only constructive, age-appropriate methods of discipline 
to guide your child’s behavior. We stress praise and encouragement of good behavior. Firm, positive statements or 
redirection are used whenever possible.  Students misbehaving will be given ONE warning.  The second offense will 
result in a time out “thinking chair”.  The third time parents will be contacted. 
• If a brief supervised separation from the group is needed, the “Thinking Chair” is used as a time-out based on one 
minute per year of child’s age. No form of physical or corporal punishment is allowed. This includes no spanking, 
slapping, pinching, kicking, poking or swatting.  In addition, children will not be allowed to hurt others or 
themselves or destroy property. Parents will be called immediately in such cases. 
• Please remember that we care about all children at ISM and will always inform you of any problems or concerns, as 
well as outstanding behavior. We always welcome your feedback at anytime.   
• Please note that all staff members at ISM are mandated reporters of child abuse/neglect.

MEALS &  SNACKS  
Students attending during the lunch hour will need to bring a packed lunch.  
Your child will be the snack helper once a month. We have a monthly snack rotation, and each child is to bring a 12- 
store-bought snacks and 12- 100% juice boxes or pouches to hand out and share once a month with their classmates.  
 
CLOSINGS  
• We follow the Marshfield Public Schools calendar.  We will be closed when Marshfield Schools are closed. 
• We will make every attempt to remain open during inclement weather, however; we will close in the case of dangerous road 
conditions or states of emergency.  
• We will close to observe the following holidays/vacations: Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New 
Year’s Day, Good Friday, Memorial Day and Independence Day. Please see our calendar for a complete list of closings. Additional 
days closed before or after Thanksgiving and Christmas may be added.  
• We will be open M-F during the Summer with fun summer programs! 
 
TERMINATION OF CARE 
A written ONE MONTH NOTICE WITH PAYMENT, is required by you for permanent withdrawal or reduction in 
enrolled days.  You may choose to not send your child during the final month, but PAYMENT IS STILL DUE.  
Refunds or credit will NOT be given for early withdraw or missed days during the program enrollment.  
 
Furthermore, we have the right to terminate the care of your child when we believe it is to the child’s benefit and/or 
required for the welfare of other children/staff in our facility.
