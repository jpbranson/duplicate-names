Mission, Vision & Values
Imagination Station is a nonprofit science center on a mission to empower everyone to explore the sciences. As Toledo’s hub for STEAM learning, we spark curiosity, fuel innovation and create inclusive spaces where science connects with community. Guided by values like collaboration, responsibility, creativity and joy, we work together to inspire lifelong learning and shape a better, brighter future for all.

Our History
A science center that inspires ingenuity and creativity has been a pillar of the Toledo region for more than 25 years. While we have changed our name, our mission, from when we opened our doors to now, has always been to empower everyone to explore the sciences.
Our timeline showcases our history and, on a grander scale, the impact of our work. However, we know that the importance of our work is seen in the day to day wonder of each of our visitors.

Inclusion
We believe equity, access and inclusion are essential to enhancing STEAM learning for all. We are dedicated to creating an environment that celebrates diversity, values accountability and prioritizes accessibility. We understand this process is ongoing and are committed to working intentionally to ensure that Science is for Everyone. Please join us in this important endeavor, as we strive to inspire a lifelong love of STEAM in our community.
