# Enterprise operator: rendered-page evidence

Source: https://peariverhistorical.square.site/
Observed September 26, 2026 in the Codex in-app browser, tab title
Home | Pea River Historical & Genealogical Society.
The cached HTML is a JavaScript application shell; the following facts came from
the rendered page's visible About, Location and Donate sections.

The society describes itself as a local 501(c)(3) nonprofit and says it owns and
operates **Pea River Museum** at the historic downtown Enterprise depot. It also
operates a gift shop and research library. The page gives the society contact at
108 South Main Street, Enterprise, Alabama 36330 and phone 334-393-2901. It describes
an all-volunteer organization and explains that donations support its operations.
The displayed contact-location hours are not independently identified as museum
hours, so they must not be copied into a depot visitor recommendation.

The older operator home page separately labels the Railroad Street Depot Museum
and the library/shop at 108 South Main. That distinction supports retaining the
depot museum while excluding the separate library/shop source cluster.

This was read-only research. No contact, donation, membership, subscription or
other form was submitted.
