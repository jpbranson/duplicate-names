I’d like to take this opportunity to welcome you to Mt. Vernon, The Festival Arts City!  Be sure 
to check out enjoymtvernon.com and mtvernon.com for information about events and 
happenings in and around Mt. Vernon and for municipal questions that may arise.

Our city slogan is “Creativity Redefined”. Mt. Vernon certainly is an artistic city with a creative 
nature. It is home to the accredited Cedarhurst Center for the Arts and many other traditional 
art and performing arts venues. But we don’t stop at traditional art… we show our creativity all 
over the city in numerous ways from paintings to hot rods, from live music to our animals… we 
show our creativity through the things we are passionate about. 
 
Mt. Vernon Festivals, Inc. is a non-profit 501c3 organization with a mission of Celebrating 
creativity through a series of festivals, and artisans in action to earn and maintain Mt. Vernon’s 
Festival Arts City reputation. 
 
Mt. Vernon’s festivals are known for embracing the extraordinary and providing creativity with 
a twist in Mt. Vernon, IL where creativity is constantly being redefined. 
 
We welcome you to Mt. Vernon and encourage you to live “Creativity: Redefined!” every day!  
 
 
Angela Schrum, Director

Originally a Carnegie Library, we became a district library serving three townships comprising 
Mt. Vernon, Illinois in 1899.  In 2024, the library will celebrate 125 years of history and service

The quality of life for a community begins with a feeling of safety in our homes and streets. The 
Mt. Vernon Police Department currently has forty sworn officers, seven telecommunicators and

Mt. Vernon currently has three stations manned 24/7 to protect the lives and property of the 
residents who live and work in Mt. Vernon, as well as people visiting and traveling through. The 
Mt. Vernon Fire Department holds an Insurance Service Office (ISO) rating of a Class 3. The ISO 
rating is a measurement of the effectiveness of fire protection services. Insurance companies 
base their fire insurance premiums on this rating, generally offering lower rates to communities 
with the better protection rating of a 3.

City Water Information  
Water Deposits  
All new customers are required to pay a deposit when activating services.  Deposits can be 
made at City Hall, 1100 Main Street or by phone.   
R
esidential Deposit: $100.00 
Commercial Deposit: $150.00 minimum, the amount of deposit required is based on the size of 
the meter. 
Connection and Discontinuation  Services  
The following information will be needed for new accounts: name, service address, billing 
address, telephone number, and social security number. A copy of proof of residency, lease 
agreement, or warranty deed. All service orders will be completed the next day of business. 
Same-day service is offered with an additional $150.00 fee. If you are moving and wish to  
discontinue service, notify the Billing Services office at City Hall at least 24 hours in advance. 
Please have your forwarding address available. 
Billing Information  
Every customer, residential and commercial, has a water meter. All meters are read monthly. 
All active accounts will receive a minimum bill. 
Bills are issued monthly, and payments are due in the office 16 days after the bill date. Failure 
to receive a bill or notice or being out of town does not relieve the customer of their 
responsibility to pay the bill. Please notify Billing Services if you are having problems receiving 
your bill. 
Payment Options  
• Pay in person at City Hall Mon through Fri 8 am to 5 pm.
• Place payment in drop box. Drop boxes are located at City Hall at the front door and in 
the West parking lot by flagpole. Additional drop box is in front of H ughey Funeral 
Home by mailboxes.
• By calling 1-855-336-4984. Payments can be made with a Credit or Debit Card
(Processing Fees will apply)
• Automatic Bank Draft (no additional charge)
• Pay online (Processing Fees will apply) To pay your bill online, visit our website and set 
up an online account at: www.mtvernon.com

Disconnections  
 
Water service will be disconnected for the following reasons: 
• Nonpayment of bills 
• Returned checks 
• Falsifying billing information 
• Illegal use of water 
• Refusal to allow the water department to inspect your meter, yard, or building. 
• Non-compliance with city rules and regulations 
 
To have service restored, the past due account balance must be paid in full plus a minimum 
non-refundable fee of $25.00. For reconnection after normal business hours, same-day service 
is offered for an additional $150.00 fee. 
 
Illegal Tampering  
The City of Mt. Vernon strictly prohibits tampering with sewer manhole lids or turning on/off 
any fire hydrants, water valves, or water meters and removal of lockout devices that have been 
installed by city personnel. Violators are subject to a minimum fine of $50.00 and will be 
prosecuted under applicable laws. 
 
Emergency Water Service  
The City of Mt. Vernon has personnel available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to 
correct or assist with any problems with your water service. If an emergency 
occurs after hours, please call the Mt. Vernon Police Department at 618-242-
2131. Please limit after-hour calls to emergencies only. Billing or other questions 
will be answered during normal business hours at 618-242-6819.

City Trash  & Recycling  Service Information  
 
 
The City of Mt. Vernon contracts trash, recycling, yard waste, and bulk pick up services out to 
Republic Services.  
 
Republic Services  
800-634-0215,  
Customer service hours:  
8 a.m. – 5 p.m. Monday – Friday,  
Saturday 8 a.m. – 12 p.m.  
republicservices.com 
 
Trash 
 
Trash Pick Up: All trash needs to be curbside beginning at 6:00 a.m. on pick up day. If the 
company misses your tote on pickup day, contact Republic Services 1-800-634-0215 (printed on 
tote). The city requires totes to be removed from curb within 24 hours after pick up.   
 
Holiday schedule: The following days will be observed. New Year’s Day, Martin Luther King Day, 
Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day. Trash pickup 
service will run one day late following each of these holidays. 
 
Trash Totes: Each resident will be supplied with one 96-gallon tote. Totes are to be placed at 
curbside or in alleyways with the arrow pointing forward.  Any customer wanting an additional 
tote will need to contact Republic Services. Please call 800-634-0215 for additional tote 
information. 
 
Report missing or damaged totes to Republic Services: 800-634-0215. 
 
Yard Waste  
         
Residential Yard Waste Collection: Yard waste will be collected year-round. Customers with 
regular trash service on Monday and Tuesday will have their yard waste picked up on Tuesday.  
Customers with regular trash service on Wednesday and Friday will have their yard waste 
picked up on Wednesday.  Customers with trash service on Thursday will have their yard waste 
picked up on Thursday.

Bulk Items 
 
Bulk waste pickup: Bulk items, i.e. furniture, mattresses, small appliances or  
other materials from a residential source will be picked up at the request of the customer. No 
electronics accepted.  Please call Republic Services customer service at 800-634-0215. You 
must call customer service by Wednesday to have items picked up on Friday. 
 
Construction materials: i.e. roofing materials, drywall, siding, room additions or remodeling 
material. No paint accepted.  Republic Services will offer roll-off containers for these types of 
materials in 10, 20, and 30 cubic yard containers to the residents of Mt. Vernon. Please call 
customer service at 1-800-634-0215 for more information. 
 
Recycling  
 
Recycle Totes: One tote will be supplied to each resident for recycled materials. Each resident 
will be supplied with a separate recycling tote.  Recycling totes are to be placed at curbside or 
in alleyways with the arrow pointing forward.

Homeless Shelter s 
Lifeboat Alliance 
PO Box 76 -1717 Jefferson Avenue 
Phone: 618-244-4088 
lifeboatalliance@gmail.com 
 
Lifeboat Alliance is a 501c3 not-for-profit organization with the vision of "Helping the Homeless 
Weather Life's Storms." Their mission is "To be a Christian ministry of shelter, resources, and 
support to the homeless." Lifeboat Alliance is a 33 room shelter with separate wings to serve 
men, women, and families. Target clients are people wanting to make a sustained life-change to 
long-term housing with a stable income. To learn more about Lifeboat Alliance visit 
www.lifeboatalliance.org. 
 
Family Life Shelter 
12580 N. Sparrow Lane 
Phone: 618-242-2002 
familylifemtv.com 
 
Family Life Shelter is a ministry of Family Life Church. The vision of Family Life Shelter is to put 
hope within reach. They exist to provide hope for the homeless and needy in our community. 
They do this by offering shelter, meals, resources, classes, support, and love. Their goal is to see 
people healed and restored so that they can become reestablished as successful community 
members. Family Life Shelter serves men and women who are over the age of 18.
