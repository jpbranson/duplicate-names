History Center & Art Museum
Our campus includes permanent exhibits in the Local History & Art Museum and the 1895 Railroad Passenger Station Museum, as well as special exhibits in the Jefferson Room.

Library & Archives
At Jefferson Co. History & Art Center, we are dedicated to preserving the rich history of our community for future generations. We strive to educate the public about the significance of local heritage and the stories that have shaped our town.

Events & Programs
At History & Art Center, we offer a variety of programs and events throughout the year, including lectures, workshops and more. Our mission is to educate the public and foster a sense of community through engaging historical programs and exhibits.
Southern Indiana Folk School
At Jefferson Co. History & Art Center, we strive to preserve and promote the rich history of Jefferson County. Our goal is to engage the community and provide opportunities for everyone to learn about our shared history.
