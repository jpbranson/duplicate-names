Welcome to Lincoln County Museum!
Whether you want to research local history, discover artifacts, or experience the annual Vintage Harvest, our museum is a great place to explore!
The Lincoln County Historical Society seeks to educate all who visit the museum through the collection and preservation of Lincoln County's vibrant heritage in Washington State.
