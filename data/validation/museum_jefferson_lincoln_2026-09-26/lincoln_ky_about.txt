About
Our Journey to Preserve History
Get involved!
The Lincoln County Historical Society is for those interested in exploring the local history of one of Kentucky’s original counties. We provide engaging programs, events, and resources to foster historical appreciation and community involvement.
The society originated from a shared passion among history enthusiasts who recognized the need to document and celebrate Lincoln County’s unique past. Our mission has only strengthened over the years.
We have proudly served a diverse audience, including local residents, scholars, and history buffs, offering enriching experiences that bring the past to life for all generations.
Our Core Values
At the Lincoln County Historical Society, we are guided by our commitment to community engagement, education, and preservation of local history.  Our mission is to promote, preserve and publicize the history of Lincoln County.
Community
We believe in fostering a strong sense of community by engaging residents and encouraging their participation in preserving our local history.
Education
Education is at the heart of our mission, as we strive to provide accessible learning opportunities about Lincoln County’s significant past for all ages.
Preservation
Our dedication to preservation ensures that the rich history of Lincoln County is safeguarded for future generations to appreciate and learn from.
