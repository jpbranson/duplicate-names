Museums & Local History
Explore museums, historic homes, community collections, railroad history, maritime heritage, and interpretive centers throughout Lincoln County.
Plan Ahead:
Hours, admission fees, exhibits, programs, and access may change. Confirm current information directly with each museum before visiting.
Toledo History Center
The Toledo History Center preserves and shares local history through photographs, oral histories, historic documents, newspapers, displays, and community collections.
Location:
Toledo
Phone:

Yaquina Pacific Railroad Museum
The museum preserves Toledo’s railroad and industrial history through locomotives, antique rail cars, railroad artifacts, exhibits, historic equipment, and research materials.
100 NW A Street, Toledo
Phone:
541-336-5256
Hours:
Tuesday–Saturday, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
Admission:
Free

Pacific Maritime Heritage Center
The Pacific Maritime Heritage Center shares the stories of people who lived and worked along the coast, rivers, estuaries, ports, and working waterfronts of Lincoln County.
333 SE Bay Boulevard, Newport
Phone:
541-265-7509
Hours:
Wednesday–Sunday, 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
Admission fees apply.

Burrows House Museum
This historic Queen Anne Victorian home features regional history exhibits and collections maintained by the Lincoln County Historical Society.
545 SW 9th Street, Newport
Phone:
541-265-7509
Hours:
Saturday–Sunday, 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
Lincoln County residents receive free admission.

North Lincoln County Historical Museum
The museum preserves and interprets the history of north Lincoln County through permanent exhibits, changing displays, photographs, artifacts, and educational resources.
4907 SW Highway 101, Lincoln City
Visit the museum website for current hours, exhibits, and visitor information.

Alsea Bay Interpretive Center & Waldport Heritage Museum
The center highlights Waldport-area history, Oregon Coast bridges, transportation, early communities, local industries, and life along Alsea Bay.
620 NW Spring Street, Waldport

541-563-3561
