Three Floors of Local History
The Dowagiac Area History Museum features 6,000 square feet of exhibits highlighting the history of Dowagiac, Cass County and Sister Lakes. Interactive components make the museum a destination for visitors of all ages. The museum is located just off the beautiful downtown business district-- FREE ADMISSION!
