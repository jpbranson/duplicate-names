The Cass County Historical & Genealogical Society (CCHGS) is a non-profit, tax exempt organization that was founded on February 20, 1963.  Our goal is to preserve and make available, by publications and other means, the rich and interesting history of the people and places in Cass County, not only those who passed through to other communities.
