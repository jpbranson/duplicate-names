Contacting and Visiting the Society and Museums
The Crawford County Historical Society Archive & The Crawford Store
Physical Location
The Crawford Store Boutique as well as the Society's office and archive are located at 869 Diamond Park, Meadville, PA 16335 (Please use the far left entrance closest to Walnut Street.) You can find parking along Walnut Street, North Main Street, and metered parking along Diamond Park.
For Research Requests please fill out the form on
the Research page
. General information can be directed to us using the information below.
Phone:
814.724.6080
Email:
archive@crawfordhistorical.org
Mailing Address: P.O. Box 871, Meadville, PA 16335
Hours
Wednesday through Saturday 9 am-3 pm

Mount Hope: The Baldwin Reynolds House Museum
Physical Location
The Baldwin-Reynolds House Museum is located at 639 Terrace Street in Meadville, PA, Parking is available along Lord Street behind the museum or next door at Holland Hall.
For special requests, venue rentals, events, or tours outside of our listed hours, please call or email us.
Phone:
814.724.6080
Email:
cmeiritz@crawfordhistorical.org
Hours
May through August : Thursday through Sunday 11 am - 4 pm with guided tours at 11, 12, 1, 2, and 3 pm.
September through April: Saturday and Sunday 11 am - 4 pm with guided tours at 11, 12, 1, 2, and 3 pm.
See the museum's
Google Business Listing
and
Facebook page
for updates.
Johnson Shaw Stereoscopic Museum
Physical Location
Johnson-Shaw Stereoscopic Museum is located at 423 Chestnut Street in Meadville, PA, Parking is available along Chestnut Street near the museum.
For special requests, venue rentals, events, or tours outside of our listed hours, please call or email us.
Phone:
814.724.6080
Email:
jsherretts@crawfordhistorical.org
