Chester Historical Society presents stories of Popple Dungeon
Press release
| Sep 23, 2026 |
Comments 0
The sign for Popple Dungeon Road gets stolen several times a year, leaving residents of The Dungeon with a gaping hole until the town creates a new one. This time, however, a resident wag took it upon himself to not only replace the sign, but add a bit of levity — and a not so subtle request — as well. Photo by Cynthia Prairie
P
opple Dungeon is a curious name for a road and an area of Chester. A comment by a school teacher to her students long ago is credited for the unusual name. A new book series about Popple Dungeon Road is the modern equivalent of
Little House on the Prairie
.
Stephen S. Rollins, an author who grew up in Chester — in the Dungeon specifically, will reveal some of the true stories behind his books
Unlikely Neighbors in Vermont
and
Back to Vermont
. Please join The Chester Historical Society for this interesting presentation at 2 p.m. on Sunday, Oct. 4 at the Chester Academy Building at 230 Main St in Chester.  This free event is open to all who are curious.
