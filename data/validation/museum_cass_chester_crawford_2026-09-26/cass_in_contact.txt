Staff
Heather Fouts - Executive Director
Heather became Executive Director of the Cass County Historical Society in October 2022. She has provided community arts and museum education experiences in Indiana since 2004. She is a graduate of Indiana University, holding a Bachelor of Arts degree in Humanities and a Master of Science degree in Art Education.
Heather is an avid fan of Star Wars and Indiana Jones
. She is also a talented mixed media artist.
​
hfouts@casscountyhistory.org
Jill Snyder - Research & Museum Services
Jill joined the CCHS staff in October 2023.  Jill is a Logansport, IN native. She has over 20 years of past experience in nonprofit and
relationship management.
For 18 years, Jill worked as a therapist in Miami and Cass counties. She is a graduate of Ball State, Indiana and Indiana State Universities, holding a Master of Science
degree
in Criminal Justice and Behavioral Counseling.
Jill has 12 grandchildren, including 2 great grandchildren and loves cats.
