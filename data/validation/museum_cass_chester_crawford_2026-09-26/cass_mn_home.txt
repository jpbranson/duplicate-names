THE OLDEST 
FAIR IN 
CASS COUNTY
1898
The Pillager Fair was first held in 1898 on the south shore of Sylvan Lake.  W.B. Jones, Cass County commissioner, was also the director of the Sylvan Lake Livestock Company. Four barns, sheds, and a root cellar stood on his property. This was the social center of the neighborhood.

Welcome to Cass County Historical Society in Walker, Minnesota.
The object of the Society shall be the collection, preservation and dissemination of knowledge about the history of Cass County and to relate it to the history of the State of Minnesota.
