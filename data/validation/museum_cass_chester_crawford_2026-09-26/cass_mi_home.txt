Preserving History though Community Outreach and Education
The Cass County Historical Society is a 501(c)3 non-profit organization that works toward preserving, researching, and promoting Cass County's rich heritage.  The Historical Society has made tremendous impact thanks to the help of members and volunteers in the Cass County area.  The core of our work lies in our continuous passion for preserving local history and our dedication to community outreach and education to those in our community.
We meet the third Tuesday of each month at different area locations.  Our lecture series events run from April - October, with our last event being the annual Fall Festival at the Newton House.   Please join us by supporting our efforts to educate and preserve history for future generations to enjoy!
