The Crawford County Historical Society Museum is three floors of Native American artifacts, vintage fashions, military displays and cultural items from the local region. Museum volunteers conduct guided tours or let browse on your own.  Each exhibit is carefully curated to share a story of the past.
Located just off Route 66 in Cuba, the museum grounds also host the Veterans Memorial. Known as the Recklein Commons, the museum also shares the vicinity with the Crawford County Library District and the Recklein Auditorium.
