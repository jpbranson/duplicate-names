Arts, Culture, & Museums
Donna Reed Museum & Theatre
DENISON
The Donna Reed Museum in Denison celebrates the legacy of the iconic actress and her Iowa roots. Visitors can explore exhibits featuring Donna’s awards, vintage costumes, personal scrapbooks, and letters, offering a glimpse into her life and achievements as well as those of other notable Iowans.
More Information
W.A. McHenry House
DENISON
The McHenry House is a beautiful historic home that was built in 1885 for the W.A. McHenry family. The home is now maintained by the Crawford County Historical Society & showcases the rich heritage of Denison and Crawford County.
More Information
Klondike Hotel
MANILLA
The Klondike Hotel is a historic building located in Manilla. This historic hotel was built in 1897 during the railroad boom.
More Information
Dow House Historical Site
DOW CITY
The Dow House was built from 1872-1874 by Simeon E. Dow, this 13 room country home was purchased by the Crawford County Conservation Board in 1970.  This home shows the life of a prominent upper middle class citizen and his family in the early 1870's.
More Information
The Old Fashioned Cocktail Bar & Art Gallery
DENISON
Denison's newest upscale cocktail bar, The Old Fashioned, brings a touch of sophistication with its expertly crafted Prohibition-era cocktails. On many weekends, the bar doubles as a gallery, showcasing the works of talented local artists, including stunning paintings, photography, and unique pieces of art.
