Board meetings are conducted and noticed in accordance with the Open Meetings Act (OMA), NMSA 1978, Sections 10-15-1 to 10-15-4. If you are an individual with a disability who needs a reader, amplifier, qualified sign language interpreter, or any other form of auxiliary aid or service to attend or participate in the hearing or meeting, please contact the Board administrator at least ten days prior to the meeting or as soon as possible at (505) 603-4398 or
Ivan.Ramirez-Arechiga@dps.nm.gov
. Public documents can be provided in various accessible formats. Please contact the administrator if an accessible format is needed.
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
Notice of Public Hearing
Public Notice.
The New Mexico Law Enforcement Certification Board gives notice that it will hold a public hearing at DPS’s Law Enforcement Academy, Auditorium, at 4491 Cerrillos Rd, Santa Fe, NM 87507, and via Zoom, on Tuesday through Thursday, October 27, 28, 29, 2026 from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. to AMEND 10.29.1 NMAC; REPEAL 10.29.6; ADOPT NEW RULES 10.28.8.14 NMAC, 10.28.8.15 NMAC, 10.28.9.12 NMAC, 10.28.9.13 NMAC.
Date: October 27, 28, 29, 2026
Time: 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
Location:
DPS’s Law Enforcement Academy, Auditorium
4491 Cerrillos Rd, Santa Fe, NM 87507, and via Zoom

Purpose of the Rule:
These revisions are intended to update, clarify, and improve existing regulatory language to ensure consistency, administrative clarity, and alignment with current operational and statutory requirements.
Copies of the Rule.
Copies of the rule may be viewed on the LEA website at https://www.lea.nm.gov/law-enforcement-certification-board/ and on the NM Sunshine Portal.
Statutory Authorization.
The statutory authorization for this rulemaking is set forth in NMSA 1978, Sections 29-7-4.3(H).
Permanent Rule.
The proposed rules will be a permanent rule.
Comment on the Rule.
Interested persons may comment on the proposed permanent rule either at the hearing, by submitting written statements to Ivan Ramirez-Arechiga at 4491 Cerillos Rd., P.O. Box 1628, 87504-1628, or by email at
Ivan.Ramirez-Arechiga@dps.nm.gov
. All mailed statements must be received by
October 26, 2026.
Early submission of written statements is encouraged. Interested persons may also comment in writing at the public hearing.
Reasonable Accommodation
. Individuals with disabilities who need any form of auxiliary aid to attend or participate in the public hearing, including a reader, amplifier, qualified sign language interpreter, or any form of auxiliary aid or service, are asked to contact Ivan Ramirez-Arechiga by email at
Ivan.Ramirez-Arechiga@dps.nm.gov
as soon as possible and no later than October 5, 2026. DPS requires at least ten calendar days’ advance notice to provide special accommodation.
