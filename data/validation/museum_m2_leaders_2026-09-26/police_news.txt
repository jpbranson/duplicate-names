Following in the traditions of Chiefs Wortman and
Stewart, IPM is bringing back National Night Out to Rockaway.  If you have not seen our "new digs", or if you already have been to our new expanded location, come see what is new in the last month, or even the last week.  Come join us for this national community event on August 4, 2026, 320 S. Hwy 101, Rockaway Beach, from 4-7pm.

Newsletters
The IPM Scanner
is the official newsletter of the International Police Museum at Wheeler, Oregon.  The Scanner is one way you can stay updated with our progress as a young museum, and offers a glimpse into the history of policing via interesting articles and photographs.
If you'd like to subscribe to the electronic version of
The IPM Scanner
, send an email to
scanner@internationalpolicemuseum.org
, and let us know if you prefer an electronic version or a paper version (and don't forget to your mailing address for a paper version).  And yes, we mail to overseas subscribers.
