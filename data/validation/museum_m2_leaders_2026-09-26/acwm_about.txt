Our Mission
The American Civil War Museum explores, the causes, course, and consequences
of the Civil War
The American Civil War Museum (ACWM) has established The William Francis Bartlett Medal award to recognize leadership, courage, and selfless-service. Presented annually, the Bartlett Medal furthers the ACWM commitment to the integrity in sharing history and bringing to life a past that has the power to inspire the present.
Find out more!
Our Story
The American Civil War Museum explores the most critical period in America’s history–the Civil War era. Our premier artifact collection, immersive exhibitions, and diverse programming introduce the history of soldiers and civilians locked in a struggle over the future of the nation. We invite visitors to see themselves as part of an alive and evolving history.
We maintain three unique sites:
The American Civil War Museum – Tredegar
and the
White House of the Confederacy
, both in Richmond, VA; and
The American Civil War Museum–Appomattox
, in Appomattox, VA.
The Museum’s flagship site is located at Tredegar on the banks of the James River in Richmond. ACWM also provides tours of the historic White House of the Confederacy in Richmond, where Jefferson Davis and his family resided during the American Civil War. The Museum’s third site, ACWM-Appomattox, located in Appomattox in central Virginia, examines the final year of the War and its legacies.
In 2013, The American Civil War Museum was formed by a consolidation of the American Civil War Center and the Museum of the Confederacy.  Immediately after the consolidation of the two institutions, we began plans to build our new facility which opened in May 2019 at Tredegar.  The new, award-winning museum building encases the original Tredegar Iron Works ruin wall, and features state-of-the-art storage space to protect our unparalleled collection of artifacts.

History Needs Heroes Campaign
Preserve the Past, Inspire the Future
A campaign to fund the preservation of the ACWM’s world-class collection of Civil War military and civilian artifacts totaling over 15,000 items and over 150,000 documents.
By participating in this campaign, you will play a vital role in ensuring the longevity of our collection. Your donation to our artifact preservation efforts enables us to safeguard these important pieces of history from the war that remade America.
With your support, we ignite curiosity, inspire learning, and foster a deeper understanding of our nation’s history!
