Get Ready for
Fall on the Farms
A chill is in the air, the leaves are falling, and harvest is in full swing. This can only mean one thing: fall has arrived at Old World Wisconsin! Lace up your boots, put on your best flannel, and join us this fall to crunch your way through leafy woodland trails, sip harvest brews at Wittnebel’s Tavern, help harvest in the fields, learn about historic pickling and preservation, meet our sheep and card wool, and so much more. Experience the beauty of autumn in the Kettle Moraine during your visit to
Old World.
