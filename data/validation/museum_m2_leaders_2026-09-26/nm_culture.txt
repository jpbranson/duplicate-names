El Palacio
Magazine
El Palacio
is the oldest museum magazine of its kind, first published in November 1913 by the Museum of New Mexico. Under DCA’s stewardship, the magazine continues to cover the art, culture, and history of the Southwest as reflected in the exhibits, public programs, and scholarship of the department’s eight museums.

Education
The New Mexico Department of Cultural Affairs inspires lifelong learning by connecting people with the art, history, science, and cultures of New Mexico. Through museums, historic sites, libraries, and educational programs, we provide accessible, engaging experiences that foster curiosity, creativity, cultural understanding, and meaningful connections for learners of all ages.

The Governor’s Awards for Excellence in the Arts
The Governor’s Awards for Excellence in the Arts are presented by the Governor, New Mexico Arts Commission, and New Mexico Arts division. Established in 1974, the awards celebrate the significance of art in the economic and cultural life of New Mexico. For
more than 50 years, a diverse and prestigious list of arts practitioners, supporters, and philanthropists have been honored.

New Mexico State Library Bookmobiles
The NM State Library operates three bookmobiles that provide books and information to rural and underserved citizens (offices in Cimarron, Tucumcari, and Los Lunas). Each bookmobile travels a monthly route of regularly scheduled stops (or deposit sites for library collections) and together circulate an average of 120,000 items per year.
Visit the Bookmobiles
Wonders on Wheels
The Wonders on Wheels (WoW) Mobile Museum is a specially retrofitted 38-foot RV featuring 300 square feet of interactive arts, culture, history, and science exhibits with curriculum-based programming. Each year, the WoW team travels to all 33 New Mexico counties, bringing engaging educational experiences to schools, public libraries, fairs, festivals, and community events through exhibit tours and hands-on activities.
FamilyPass
New Mexico FamilyPass provides free admission (for up to 6 people) to 16 state museums and historic sites. FamilyPasses are available at Public Libraries throughout the state for checkout. Patrons may borrow a pass, which circulates like other library materials, for up to one week.
