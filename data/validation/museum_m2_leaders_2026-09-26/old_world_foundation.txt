About OWF
Preserving the Historical Integrity of Old World Wisconsin.
Donate Now !
Our Mission
Enriching lives through the preservation and celebration of history, Old World Foundation is dedicated to responsibly funding the advancement of Old World Wisconsin.
Our Purpose
Old World Foundation's purpose is to raise money and increase awareness of Old World Wisconsin.
Our Vision
A future where lessons of the past ignite curiosity, inspire learning, and foster a deeper understanding of our diverse cultural heritage through impactful giving.
Our History
Friends
of Old World Wisconsin began in 1984, as a group of volunteers dedicated to raising money and helping the museum in all ways possible. We grew into a dynamic fundraising organization, changing our name to Old World Wisconsin Foundation in 2005.
We are proud to remain the local, independent 501(c)(3) non-profit charitable organization. Through the help of our supporters, we have raised over $10 million to support projects and programs at Old World Wisconsin.
Did You Know?
Old World Wisconsin is Wisconsin's museum of 19th century rural life where visitors rediscover the character of America. We invite you to Explore Old World Wisconsin!
