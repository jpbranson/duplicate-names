More than just a collection of airworthy warbirds and historical artifacts, the Commemorative Air Force Minnesota Wing is a place where
you
can experience history up close.
​With hands-on displays, Living History Flight Experiences, WWII Hangar dances, and our motor pool, you can do more than just read about the history of the Greatest Generation, you can immerse yourself in it.
Come and Experience History with Us
History Flights
﻿
Take a once in a life time  experience flying in one of our authentic World War II aircraft! No piloting experience is necessary!
Learn More
Hangar dances
We host two WWII style swing dances in our museum each year.  You can join us in June and September each year.  ​​

Become a Member
As an all-volunteer 501c3 non-profit organization, our members donate their talent, labor, time and financial support to help our mission.
​For those who have a passion for history, aviation, or just helping out a good cause, the Commemorative Air Force Minnesota Wing is a great place to visit or volunteer!
