Welcome to the website of                   the CAF Lobo Wing!
Please take a few moments to wander through the site’s pages, where you’ll find lots of interesting aviation information. The CAF Lobo Wing is a unit of the Commeorative Air Force, a non‐profit, member organization which restores, flies and exhibits World War II era aircraft. The Lobo Wing is based at the airport in Moriarty, New Mexico (less than 40 miles east of Albuquerque).
If you like what you see, please come vist us at the Moriarty Airport, Hangar 80 any Saturday between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM. We sponsor a “Burger Burn” from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM for a donation as well.
