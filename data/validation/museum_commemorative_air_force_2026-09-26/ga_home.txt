Ride in our Warbirds!
Airbase Georgia offers a variety of living history flight experiences in our aircraft.  Feel the g-forces from the 1,500 horsepower Rolls Royce Merlin engine in our P-51 Mustang “Red Nose”, or transport yourself back to the Battle of Midway in the gunner’s seat of our ultra-rare SBD-5 Dauntless!  You will see, hear, and feel what the airmen of the Greatest Generation experienced in the skies over Europe and the Pacific during World War II.  Get the adrenaline rush of a lifetime and help us
Keep ‘Em Flying!
Book Now!

Aerobatic Flights Available!
Strap on your parachute and buckle into the cockpit of our LT-6 Mosquito for an adrenaline-filled flight of a lifetime!  You will experience wingovers, loops, barrel rolls, and aileron rolls just like the cadets training for combat in World War II.
Makes an unforgettable gift!

Join CAF Airbase Georgia
The Commemorative Air Force has the largest collection of flying warbirds, but our members are our greatest asset.  As a CAF Colonel, you can help fulfill our mission to
Educate, Inspire, and Honor
through the preservation and operation of our amazing aircraft.  You don’t have to be a pilot or a mechanic.  Everyone has something to offer, including you!  Want to be part of this great mission?  Join the CAF and help us keep ’em flying!

Welcome
The
Commemorative Air Force
was founded to acquire, restore and preserve in flying condition a complete collection of combat aircraft which were flown by all military services of the United States, and selected aircraft of other nations, for the education and enjoyment of present and future generations of Americans. Since 1987 the CAF Airbase Georgia has displayed its collection of vintage World War II era aircraft at numerous airshows throughout the Southeast.
We are open to the public on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am-4pm, and the first Sunday of each month 1pm-5pm
