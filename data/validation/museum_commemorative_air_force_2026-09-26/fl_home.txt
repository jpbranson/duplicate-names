Hangar and Library open to the public most Saturday's from 10:00AM to 12:00 PM (otherwise contact us for arrangements).
The mission of the CAF is education, such that generations of Americans will value and support the contributions of military aviation in assuring our nation's freedom. Without the gracious support of the Volusia County Cultural Committee, funding for our education programs at the CAF Florida Wing would not be possible. We are deeply grateful for their generosity and continued commitment to cultural enrichment.
