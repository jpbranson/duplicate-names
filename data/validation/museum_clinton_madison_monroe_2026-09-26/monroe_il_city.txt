House of Neighborly Service
Notes
How can HNS help you? We can provide support and resources to Monroe County residents in need of the following:
Food and personal hygiene items
Utilities Assistance
Rent and mortgage assistance
Clothing
Thanksgiving meal baskets
Christmas gifts for under 18 or in school
Job searches, resumes, job coaching
Financial budget planning
Senior apartment pantry assistance
School lunch grants
Summer lunch programs
Personal needs and more!!
Our volunteers are trained and willing to listen and discuss appropriate ways in which HNS can best assist you! Please call our office for an appointment and we will be happy to help you find the best solution to your situation. We look forward to hearing from you!

Notes
Our meetings are held on the third Thursday of each month at the Acorns Golf Links. There is a social hour at 5:30pm before the meeting and dinner at 6:30pm.
The Waterloo Lions are a very active club. Our organization’s moto is “We Serve”.  Please feel free to attend a meeting and learn about the many way we serve our community and beyond.

Waterloo’s Emergency Notification System
The City of Waterloo is proud to offer citizens a means of receiving emergency notification calls. By registering your information in the City’s database you will receive calls from the City of Waterloo. You will also have the option to receive tornado, flash flood and/or severe thunderstorm notifications from the National Weather Service.
