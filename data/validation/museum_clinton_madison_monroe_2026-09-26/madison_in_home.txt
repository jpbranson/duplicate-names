Made In Madison County
For over 100 years Madison County was an industrial center which produced a wide variety of items that sold across the country.  General Motors automotive parts, Sellers kitchen cabinets, Aladdin lamps, Wright “brilliant” cut glass, Red Gold canned tomatoes (still in business), and L & M musical instrument straps (still in business) are just some of the products spotlighted in this 2,000 square foot state-of-the-art open display area.

Our Exhibits Are FREE
Exhibits in the
Museum of Madison County History
are free of charge to everyone.
Of course, we appreciate monetary donations after you have seen our attractive, comprehensive, and diverse displays illustrating our local heritage.  In this way, we educate and inspire the public, which fulfills our mission statement, and you, the visitor, can participate in the preservation of our local history.
If you have a group that would like to come to the museum, please contact us to make an appointment. For more information or if you have questions, please call us at (765) 683-0052 during business hours.
