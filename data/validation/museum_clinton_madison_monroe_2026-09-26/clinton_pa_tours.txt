YOUR CART
Tours
Individuals and families are encouraged to stop in for a personalized tour. As you step into the past, you experience the Victorian Era, learning about our lumbering industry, the area canals, and antique tools used over 100 years ago.
Tour rates are $3 per person, members are free. Donations thankfully accepted.
There is no need to make an appointment to tour the Heisey House Museum,
​but groups of 8 or more must call for an appointment.
Hours of operation:
​Tuesday - 10 am to 3 pm
Thursday - 10 am to 3 pm
Group Tours
The Clinton County Historical Society invites travel agents and tour bus operators to consider visiting our beautiful Central Pennsylvania Region. We will create a wonderful day-long experience, starting with a tour of the Historical Society's Heisey House Museum and Ice House, with the option of progressing to our other properties including the Barton Street School, Farrandsville Iron Furnace, and Castanea Railroad Station.
Please call
570-748-7254
or e-mail
[email protected]
in advance to book your tour of 8 or more; all our group guided tours begin at the Heisey House Museum and are customized to meet your needs. Most tours of the house take about two hours. We welcome picnic lunches on the grounds, which are equipped with picnic tables. We encourage people to visit our gift shop. If you decide not to tour our other properties, we encourage you to explore nearby downtown Lock Haven, visit the Piper Aviation Museum, or just take a stroll along the river and enjoy the beautiful scenery of Central Pennsylvania.
Group tour admission is $3 per person with complimentary admission for tour leaders and bus drivers. ​
School Tours
We encourage participation by area school districts and work closely with educators to offer the children a memorable and educational experience of their visit to our Society. As with group tours, we customize your tour and ask you call ahead to make reservations.
