The Barton Street School
The Barton Street School House is a one room schoolhouse dating from 1874.  It was transferred to the Historical Society in 1995 and houses memorabilia from local fire companies, early 20
th
century Lock Haven history, and the military.
​
Address: 102 Barton Street, Lock Haven, PA
