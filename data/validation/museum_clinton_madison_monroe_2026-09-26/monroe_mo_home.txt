About Monroe County Historical Society
Our Mission and Activities
The Monroe County Historical Society is dedicated to preserving the rich history of Monroe County, Missouri. We collect and maintain historical documents, artifacts, and written history to educate future generations.
Visit our society headquarters at the Nancy Stone Research Center at 124 West Caldwell St. in Paris or our Museum at the Monroe County Courthouse.
