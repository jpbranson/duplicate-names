About the Clinton County History Center
The Clinton County History Center is home to the Clinton County Historical Society and the Clinton County Genealogical Society. Together, we preserve and share the artifacts, documents, photographs and stories that tell Clinton County’s history – through exhibits, research services and community programs.

FamilySearch Affiliate Library
The Clinton County History Center is proud to be a
FamilySearch Affiliate Library
, providing patrons with expanded access to genealogy records and resources from one of the world’s largest family history organizations.
Affiliate access is available
in person
at our Genealogical Society Research Library during public hours or by appointment. Visit us to explore these tools and receive personalized assistance from our knowledgeable staff and volunteers.
