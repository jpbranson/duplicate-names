The M4 distinction is between an institution's own governance and independence from
other museums. A locally independent society can still operate multiple distinct
museum institutions. Archives, administrative offices, membership networks, grants,
monuments and rooms/buildings within one museum do not automatically establish a
museum chain. Apply evidence to identified records, not all same-name societies.
