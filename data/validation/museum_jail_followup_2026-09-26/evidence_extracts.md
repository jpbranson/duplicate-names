# Evidence extracts and access limits

These are factual paraphrases, not complete copies of the linked sources.

- [Georgia corporate filing, March 11, 2019](https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov/BusinessSearch/DownloadFile?filingNo=16843804): control H307912, Barnesville Lamar County Historical Society Inc., domestic nonprofit; principal office and registered agent at 858 Thomaston Street. This matches the IMLS mailing address. The PDF was readable through the web tool; native cache returned 403. A corporate filing identifies the office, not a public museum location.
- [Lawrence County's operator link](https://lawrencecountytn.gov/county-services/get-involved/) leads to the [society page](http://home.lorettotel.net/~lcarchives/lchistsoc.htm). The February 17, 2024 update identifies Old Jail on Waterloo Street, local officers/directors, membership/donation funding and county/city assistance. Current county attractions independently names the museum. Native cache succeeded; web HTTPS upgrade failed. Source Overture cellblock7.org is unrelated.
- [Greenwood hours](https://www.greenwoodmuseumonline.com/Museum-Information/Hours-of-Operation): operator currently requires an appointment; do not publish older regular hours. [Location](https://www.greenwoodmuseumonline.com/Museum-Information/Our-Location) says 307 East Town Square, southeast corner. Historic-property pages explain that cabin/school buildings moved into the historic district, but full institution/campus scope still requires review.
- [Sanders County 2022 minutes](https://www.co.sanders.mt.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/ArchivedMinutes/_01042022-13), PDF page 22: the county agreed to museum repair funding following a representative's request. This does not itself establish museum governance. The [state tourism entry](https://visitmt.com/listing/old-jail-museum-14188) links a Facebook operator page that returned an access block.
- [Franklin County October 2023 minutes](https://www.franklincotn.us/assets/pdfs/agendas_minutes_commission/minutes/2023/10162023.pdf): county authorized structural restoration work on Old Jail. This is stronger current building evidence than the older NPS nomination but does not resolve the separate museum and society nonprofit identities.
