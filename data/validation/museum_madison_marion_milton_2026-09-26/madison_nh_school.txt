THIS IS WHAT HAS BEEN ACCOMPLISHED SO FAR:
May 2023    Lead and asbestos testing was arranged with Desmarais Environmental by School Board.
July 2023    Demarais' report determined there is lead on the building and in the surrounding soil.
Aug 2023    Madison Corner School Preservation Committee (MCSPC) was formed.
Sep 2023    MCSPC submitted application to the NH Preservation Alliance's Seven to Save program.
Oct 2023    The Corner School did not make the Seven to Save listing.
Mar 2024    The Madison School District voted 308 to 126 in favor of raising & appropriating $25,000
to be placed in the Madison Schoolhouse Capital Reserve Fund.
May 2024    MCSPC partnered with the Madison Historical Society, thus sharing the 501c3 nonprofit
status, in order to begin fundraising and accepting donations.
Dec 2024    Attended the Madison School PTO Holiday Fair to raise awareness. $41 donations.
Mar 2025    Madison School District voted 354 to 223 to raise & appropriate another $25,000.
Apr 2025    Began exploring costs to address deteriorating exterior. (Local estimate was $20,000.)
Jun 2025    Submitted grant application for $23,000 to NH Land & Community Heritage Investment
Program (LCHIP).
Jul 2025    Removed all Madison School theater costumes which were stored in schoolhouses to a
storage shed. Removed and donated all stored surplus school equipment.
Aug 2025    Resubmitted application to NH Preservation Alliance's Seven to Save program.
Attended Old Home Week Community Fair & raised $418.
Held Corner School Open House, had 50 visitors, $330.
Sep 2025    Held Corner School Open House, had 50 visitors, raised $126.
Oct 2025    Corner School chosen as a Seven to Save listee.
Nov 2025    $23,000 LCHIP grant declined in part to lacking a Qualified Preservation Plan (QPP) by
a Historic Building Consultant.
Hired Snell Brothers to clean mildewed ceiling and insulation material from basement
.
Dec 2025 - Applied for a Preservation Assessment Grant and was awarded $4200.
Started arranging for a Historic Building Consultant to create a QPP.
Attended the Madison School PTO Holiday Fair and raised $364.

LOCATION HISTORY
*    The Madison Corner School was one of nine
one room schoolhouses in Madison.
*    It was built in 1835 on Rt. 113 across from
Mooney Hill Road.
*    It was first moved in 1873 to property given
by James J. Burke which is the current school
property. The purpose of the move was to be
more centrally located to the children who
traveled
the furthest.
*    In 1979 the building was moved to its current
location in front of the Madison Elementary
School
and onto a cellar foundation to allow for
additions to the present school.
PURPOSE
*    The Madison Corner School served as a 1st -
12th grade one room school. The 1860 school
registry lists 25 boys and 28 girls ranging in age
from 4-18.
*    In 1922 the Madison High School was built. It
closed in 1949.
*    The Madison Corner School served elementary
students until 1950 when Madison began sending
high school students to Kennett in Conway and
all elementary students moved to the old high
school building.
*    The Corner School served as the Town and
School Library until the town library was built
in 1994 and the elementary school library in
2000. Since then, it has served as a music room,
Colonial Days and most recently storage for the
Madison Theater Group.
HISTORIC REGISTERS
​The Madison Corner School was accepted onto the
National Register of Historic Places in 1980 and
the New Hampshire State Register of Historic
places in 2006.
CURRENT STATE OF BUILDING
Through the years the MCS has slowly fallen into
a state of neglect and disrepair. Most
recently a
basement sump pump failed, causing
water to
collect, causing extensive damage to
the
basement ceiling and insulation. Other
concerns:
rotted windowsills, broken windows,
peeling
paint and rotted siding, lead paint and
lead
contaminated soil.

MADISON CORNER SCHOOL HOUSE
COMMITTEE

PRESERVATION OF THE MADISON CORNER

OF THIS LOCAL TREASURE.
