Built on a commitment
to passing on stories, preserving heritage, and building community
The Marion County Historical Society was founded in 1969 to nurture interest in the history of Marion County, Ohio.
The Society purchased Marion’s historic Old Post Office, now known as Heritage Hall. Since 1989, Heritage Hall has been the Society’s home. This National Register landmark serves as our headquarters and museum, and it is also home to the Wyandot Popcorn Museum, the only popcorn museum in the world.
The Marion County Historical Society is deeply rooted in our community and dedicated to our mission of collecting, preserving, and sharing the rich history of Marion County. We offer something for everyone and take great joy in educating our visitors. We honor the people and industries that helped shape our town and are here to ensure that their stories are not forgotten. While we celebrate those who came before us, we are equally committed to ensuring their stories are passed on to future generations.

The Marion County museum offers a one-of-a-kind experience that celebrates local history through engaging exhibits and interactive displays. As the heart of the Marion County historical museum, Heritage Hall preserves the stories and artifacts that shaped our community.

Wyandot Popcorn Museum
Step under the vibrant circus tent inside Heritage Hall and discover the world’s only popcorn museum — an unforgettable experience for families, history buffs, and popcorn lovers alike.
Home to the most extensive and beautifully restored collection of popcorn wagons and peanut roasters in the United States, the museum showcases rare, turn-of-the-century artifacts that celebrate a unique slice of Americana.
Heritage Hall features a rich collection of memorabilia that reflects the people, industries, and spirit that helped Marion County leave its mark on the world.

Education Programs
The Marion County Historical Society offers hands-on, interactive education programs for all ages. Visitors can explore Marion County history through workshops, guided tours, and special presentations, including the chance to hear original
speeches by Warren G. Harding
.
Young learners can also dive into the
Marion County Historical Society Activity Book
, available for purchase at Heritage Hall.
With more engaging programs on the way, there’s always something new to discover, making history both educational and exciting for students, families, and lifelong learners.
