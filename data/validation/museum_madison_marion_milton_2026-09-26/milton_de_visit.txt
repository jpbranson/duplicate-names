Location and directions:
Milton Historical Society + Museum
210 Union Street, Milton, DE 19968
From the intersection of DE-16 & Union St/DE-5:
Go south on Union Street 0.6 miles. The museum is on the right, about a block beyond King's Ice Cream.
From Coastal Highway/DE-1:
Turn west onto DE-16, go 2.6 miles to the intersection of Union St/DE-5 and DE-16. Turn south onto Union Street for 0.6 miles, museum will be on the right about a block beyond King's Ice Cream.
From US 50E toward Ocean City:
Turn left onto MD-404, turn left onto DE-16 (East) to the intersection of Union Street/DE-5 and DE-16. Turn onto Union Street, going into town, for about 0.6 miles. Museum will be on the right about a block beyond King's Ice Cream.
