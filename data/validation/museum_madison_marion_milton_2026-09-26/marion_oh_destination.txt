Heritage Hall
Heritage Hall
Gather & Meet
169 East Church Street, Marion, OH 43302
(740) 387-4255
Visit Website
Heritage Hall is a circa 1910 former post office. Housed in Heritage Hall are the Marion County Historical Society and the Wyandot Popcorn Museum.
The auditorium at Heritage Hall can accommodate 50-75 people. The venue includes kitchen facilities with a refrigerator, coffee pot, sink, and microwave. There are two restrooms located within the auditorium and ample parking. Tables and chairs, as well as a pull-down screen for presentations, are included with the rental. The venue is handicap-accessible and available for rent by reservation. To add a unique twist to your event, ask about adding a museum tour.
Current Hours of Operation:
Regular hours are May-October:
Monday and Tuesday: CLOSED
Wednesday-Sunday: 1-4pm
November: Saturday and Sunday Only: 1-4pm
Closed December-April.
