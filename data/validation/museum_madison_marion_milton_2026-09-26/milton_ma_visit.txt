Visit Us
The Milton Historical Society is located in
the Suffolk Resolves House
, 1370 Canton Avenue, Milton MA.
The Suffolk Resolves House is open to the public on designated days during the year.
Please check out
Event Calendar
for additional opportunities to visit Suffolk Resolves House.
Parking at Suffolk Resolves House
Please park perpendicularly along the private road leading to our driveway.
Please do not park on Canton Avenue as emergency vehicle access must be maintained.

ABOUT
Established in 1904, the Milton Historical Society is a non-profit organization dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich history of Milton. Housed in the Suffolk Resolves House, the Society offers a range of programs, exhibits, and resources bringing the town's history to life.
