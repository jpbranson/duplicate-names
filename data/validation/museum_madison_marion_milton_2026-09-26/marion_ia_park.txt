Discover Marion County Park in Knoxville
The 140 acre “County Park” is MCCB’s oldest recreation area. Its rolling hills contain many opportunities for recreational enjoyment. A campground with 99 campsites, shower/restroom facilities and picnic shelters provide for a summer family retreat. A 7 acre fishing lake, provides an excellent location to fish for large mouth bass, bluegill and catfish. Picnic areas with playground equipment, flush restrooms and picnic shelters are available for daily usage. A basketball court, baseball and softball fields are also located in the park.
Additionally, the park has a diverse array of scenic backdrops; including, a fishing pier, covered bridge, and natural foliage settings that are utilized by photographers for inspirational, family and senior class pictures.
The Marion County Historical Society maintains a historical village at the park, complete with a log cabin, museum, country schoolhouse, church, general store and stagecoach inn; that are open for display on weekends from Memorial Day to Labor Day.
Locals have known and travelers are learning that, Marion County Park is a destination for many recreational enthusiasts.

Marion County Park Campground
has 99 campsites. Campsite amenities range from 50 Amp electric with water
to 50 Amp full service with sewer. The campground also contains a modern shower building, one additional flush
restroom, picnic shelter and campground host on site.
Marion County Park Campground Map
In the event of freezing conditions, the water to the campsites may not be on.
Water will be available at a hydrant to fill tanks and the shower building will be available for use.
Please note that the camping fees will not change in the event the water is off.
