Milton House Signature Event
The 2026 Milton House Museum Signature Event will take place on September 26, 2026, at the Janesville Performing Arts Center in Janesville.
This special evening will feature an opening performance by
Ko-Thi Dance Company
of Milwaukee, followed by a main performance from the renowned
Fisk Jubilee Singers
of Fisk University in Tennessee.
For additional event details, and to purchase tickets, please visit our Signature Event page by clicking on the button below.
