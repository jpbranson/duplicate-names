WELCOME TO OUR NEW SITE!
The Marion County Historical Society is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization comprised of individuals who are passionate about the preservation of the rich heritage of Marion County, Alabama. The primary focus of our activities is the preservation of historical sites, artifacts, data, photos, documents, or any other item which would serve to preserve our history for future generations.
