Our Mission & Vision
OUR MISSION
The Milton Historical Society strives to preserve and disseminate Milton's history, while using the Milton House Museum and its role on the loose network known as the Underground Railroad, as a vehicle to educate on how our understanding of history informs how we view our nation today.
OUR VISION
Establish the Milton House Museum as the premier experiential historic site for Civil Rights education in the state of Wisconsin
