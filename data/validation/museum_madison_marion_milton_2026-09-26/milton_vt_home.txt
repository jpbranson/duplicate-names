Please join us as we partner with Colchester and Georgia Historical Societies to bring this exciting 2-day event. Scan the QR Code for more information.
Town Historian, Jim Ballard will give a presentation from 10:00 to Noon on Saturday,
September 26th
at the Milton Museum about Milton's role in the Civil War.
Due to unforeseen circumstances, the Vermont Early Music Project had to cancel their performance Saturday afternoon at 4:30. There is nothing scheduled to fill this time slot at this time.
​1815 Log Schoolhouse
Saturday, September 26, 10 am - 2 pm
Airport Park, Colchester Point Road, Colchester:
Stop by the 1815 Log Schoolhouse at Airport Park for this special day beyond its regular summer season. The Colchester Historical Society is participating as a proud co-sponsor with the Milton and Georgia Vermont Historical Societies in a 250th American birthday celebration honoring the sacrifice and heritage of three Vermont communities during a time of challenge.
Georgia
- Colin Conger will be doing a presentation at the Stannard Monument on Route 7 in Georgia at 9:30 Saturday morning, Sept 26. Have you wondered about that monument you see along Route 7, north of town? Come learn more about it.   Please be respectful of Betty Dunsmore’s property for parking. The brick schoolhouse museum will be open 10am to 2pm this Saturday. Special hours. Time is running out to get in to see the museum this year. Stop by and visit on Sat.
